# Neumorphic glass-fusion polish

## What will change
- Unify results cards with a clearer header rhythm, quieter borders, and layered glass-neumorphic depth.
- Give charts subtle inset canvases, more legible labels, and stable mobile dimensions without changing data or interactions.
- Refine compact and expanded sidebar labels, active states, spacing, and transitions across desktop and mobile.
- Apply the same surface, spacing, control, and emphasis system to Pricing and Sign in while preserving every existing action.

## Visual direction
- Keep WebVision's lavender, white, and purple identity.
- Use restrained translucent surfaces and soft inset depth rather than heavy shadows.
- Reserve green, orange, and red for status meaning.
- Keep motion short and subtle, with accessible focus states and touch targets.

## Technical details
- Extend existing semantic design tokens and shared glass/neumorphic utilities.
- Reuse those utilities in the relevant result, chart, sidebar, pricing, and auth presentation code.
- Preserve current business logic, routes, authentication, payment behavior, and chart data.
- Verify desktop and narrow mobile layouts for overflow, overlap, and readable chart labels.
